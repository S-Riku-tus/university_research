## Interpretation

- `batch_size=64` is better for overall regression and binary ONB detection in
  this 3-fold confirmation run.
- `batch_size=48` gives a smaller mean ONB-neighborhood RMSE, but its binary
  metrics are weaker and fold-level behavior is less useful for detection.
- The strongest near-term default is therefore
  `cnntf_v2_dim256_attn`, `lr=0.0001`, `batch_size=64`.
- ONB-local error is still unstable, especially because the `batch_size=64`
  run had one fold with large ONB RMSE. The next validation should check other
  experiment dates before making a broad claim.
