\chapter*{要旨}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{要旨}
本研究は，ナトリウム冷却高速炉の炉心局所異常に伴う冷却材沸騰の早期把握に資する音響監視技術に向け，背景雑音下でも核沸騰開始（ONB）近傍を少ない見逃し・誤報で捉え，判断根拠を説明できる検知手法へ，熱流束回帰に基づく従来手法を発展させることを目的とする．その基礎検討として，水のプール沸騰音響から熱流束を回帰し，日別のONB熱流束を閾値として判定する方法に，異なる入力表現を用いるモデル統合を適用した．全熱流束域の回帰誤差と開始付近の判定を対応づけ，目的に対する改良と不足を調べた．

既存の2025年6月11日・6月18日の計36録音から，各録音60個の1秒区間を作成し，45区間を学習，15区間を評価に用いた．評価は540区間であり，未知録音の評価ではない．3\,kHzを上限とするSTFT powerを用いる従来3モデルに，34個の音響要約特徴を用いるHistGradientBoostingとExtraTreesを追加した．無雑音学習側のout-of-fold予測で重みを求め，モデル，前処理および重みを無雑音と6段階の固定基準水流音付加条件で共通にした．全域回帰誤差，見逃し・誤報，ONB近傍誤差，日別の検知到達熱流束を比較した．

5モデル統合の全域RMSEは，MSE基準の従来3モデル統合に対して全7条件で小さく，無雑音で34.07から27.80\,\kw{}，基準SNR $-20$\,dBで81.14から75.23\,\kw{}となった．総見逃し件数もそれぞれ42から27，65から52へ減少した．一方，無雑音から$-20$\,dBへのRMSE増加量は47.07から47.43\,\kw{}となり，この比較では悪化量の縮小は確認されなかった．記録上最初のONB段階に含まれる30評価区間のうち，5モデル統合が陽性としたのは無雑音8区間，$-20$\,dBで0区間であり，開始段階の十分な検知には至らなかった．

保存予測の誤差分解から，統合の利得には過大・過小予測の相殺に加え，同方向に外れた予測の誤差縮小が含まれた．共通入力の時間行並べ替えではCNN系が変化し，要約特徴を用いる2モデルは不変であり，入力依存の違いを確認した．ただし，この違いが実際の水流雑音下の利得を生む因果割合や，利用帯域の物理音源は確定していない．以上から，現在のデータ範囲では統合による全域回帰と判定の部分的な改善を示せる一方，ONB発生段階の検知，未知日への適用および物理的な根拠の確定が残る．

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
To support the future development of acoustic monitoring for coolant boiling associated with local core anomalies in sodium-cooled fast reactors, this study aims to advance a heat-flux-regression-based method toward onset-of-nucleate-boiling (ONB) detection under background noise with fewer missed detections and false alarms and with explainable acoustic evidence. As a fundamental study using water pool-boiling acoustics, model integration with different input representations was examined. Existing recordings from June 11 and June 18, 2025 were divided into one-second segments. Forty-five segments per recording were used for training and fifteen for evaluation, giving 1,620 training and 540 evaluation segments from 36 recordings. This design evaluates unused segments of known recordings rather than unseen recording days.

Two regressors using 34 acoustic summary features, HistGradientBoosting and ExtraTrees, were added to three existing models. The models, preprocessing and integration weights fitted on clean training data were fixed across clean and six reference-level water-flow-noise conditions. The five-model integration reduced overall RMSE relative to the MSE-weighted original-three integration in all seven conditions, from 34.07 to 27.80 kW/m\textsuperscript{2} in the clean condition and from 81.14 to 75.23 kW/m\textsuperscript{2} at a reference SNR of $-20$ dB. However, the clean-to-noisy RMSE increase was not reduced. Only 8 of 30 evaluation segments at the recorded ONB stages were detected in the clean condition, and none at $-20$ dB. These 30 segments came from two recordings and do not represent 30 independent onset events.

Residual analysis showed that error reduction involved both cancellation of opposite-sign errors and reduction within same-sign error groups. Common input perturbations also demonstrated different dependencies on within-segment temporal arrangement. These findings support partial improvements within the evaluated data, while adequate detection at the recorded onset stage, generalization to unseen days, and identification of physical sound sources remain unresolved.
